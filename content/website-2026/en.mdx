## 1. Domain Registration

A domain is your website's address on the internet, for example:

- mywebsite.com
- mycompany.com

Registering a domain typically costs around **US$10 to US$20 per year**, depending on the registrar and the domain extension you choose.

This is an annual fee required to keep your domain active.
